Introduction to Content Management Systems (3 hours)

Unit objectives

This short unit gives students a practical overview of content management systems so they can compare CMS platforms with custom framework-based development and choose the right approach for a project.

Learning outcomes

  • Define a CMS and explain its main components.
  • Compare WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla at a practical level.
  • Identify projects that are better suited to a CMS than to custom code.
  • Explain how CMS knowledge complements framework-based development.

Coverage checklist

  • CMS concept and architecture overview
  • WordPress, Drupal, Joomla comparison
  • When CMS is best vs custom web development
  • How CMS knowledge complements frameworks

Classroom focus

The aim is not to turn students into CMS administrators, but to help them recognize the workflow, extension model, and content-editing strengths of CMS platforms when compared with handcrafted applications.
